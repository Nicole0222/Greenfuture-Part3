About GreenFuture NGO

Working together to protect our environment.

Our Story

GreenFuture was founded in 2020 by environmental scientists and community leaders in Johannesburg. We saw the need for practical, community-driven solutions to environmental problems and decided to take action.

Our Mission

To restore and protect the natural environment through community action, education, and sustainable development projects that benefit both people and the planet.


Our Vision

A South Africa where every community has access to clean air, green spaces, and the knowledge to live sustainably.

Our Values

Sustainability

Long-term solutions over quick fixes.

Community

Working with people, not for people.

Education

Knowledge is the key to lasting change.
