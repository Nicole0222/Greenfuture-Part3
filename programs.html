What is GreenFuture's mission in Johannesburg?

We promote sustainable development, clean energy, and food security across Johannesburg and Gauteng province.

How to volunteer for programs?

Visit our enquiry page to calculate costs and check availability. Programs cost R50 per person.

Where are programs held?

Main office in Johannesburg CBD, with sites in Soweto, Alexandra, and Pretoria. See map on About page.

What is the cost?

Each program is R50 per person. Use our enquiry calculator to estimate total cost based on quantity.
