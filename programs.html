Our Programs

Practical projects creating environmental and community impact.

Program 1: Urban Tree Planting

We partner with municipalities and residents to plant indigenous trees in public spaces, schools, and streets. This helps reduce heat, improve air quality, and create shade. Each tree is tracked and maintained for 3 years.

Program 2: Environmental Education

Our team visits primary and high schools to run interactive workshops on recycling, water conservation, and biodiversity. We provide free learning materials and help schools start eco-clubs.

Program 3: Community Clean-Ups

Monthly clean-up drives in rivers, parks, and streets. We also run waste management training and help communities set up recycling points to reduce litter.
